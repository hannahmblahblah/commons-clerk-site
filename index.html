Questions

Do you replace our management company?

No. We're for boards that manage themselves. We don't collect dues, pay vendors, hold bank access or do accounting.

Is this legal advice?

No. We draft documents from your governing documents and your board's decisions. For questions about state law, enforcement, fines, liens, collections or disputes, talk to your association's attorney.

Who sends things to owners?

Your board. We give you drafts. Your board approves and sends them, or we send from a board-owned address only with written permission.

How do you use AI?

AI writes the first draft and a person checks every document against your notes and governing documents before you see it. Model training is turned off in the AI tools we use, and we only use the minimum owner information needed.

What do you need from us?

Your governing documents, the board roster, and a recording or notes after each meeting.

Do we have to meet on video?

No. Everything works by email and shared folders.

Can we cancel?

Yes, anytime. You keep everything we made.
