HOA meeting minutes template

A blank set of minutes for a board meeting, an annual meeting, or a special meeting. It has a place for the call to order, quorum, each motion, the vote, the action items, and adjournment.

Use this to write down what happened. It is not legal advice, and it does not say what your association is required to record. Follow your governing documents and state law, including the Colorado Common Interest Ownership Act (CCIOA) where it applies, and ask your attorney when you are unsure.

What to include

  • Call to order. Name the person who opened the meeting and the time on the clock.
  • Quorum. List the directors present and absent, and whether a quorum was present under your bylaws. If the chair says there was no quorum, write that down and do not record ordinary business as decided.
  • Motions. Write the motion in the words that were voted on, who moved it, who seconded it, and the vote. "The board discussed the roof" does not record a decision.
  • Action items. Each task needs a person and a due date, or it will not get done.
  • Adjournment. Record the time the meeting ended.
Example of one motion

Item. Landscaping contract

Motion. "Approve the [vendor] proposal for $[amount], to be paid from the operating account."

Moved by [name]. Seconded by [name].

Vote. [4] for, [0] against, [1] abstain ([name]). Result. Motion passed.

1. Call to order

[Name, role] called the meeting to order at [time].

2. Attendance and quorum

Directors present: [names]

Directors absent: [names]

Also present: [owners or guests, or "none"]

Quorum: A quorum [was / was not] present under the bylaws.

3. Approval of the agenda

Motion. Approve the agenda [as presented / with these changes: ...].

Moved by [name]. Seconded by [name].

Vote. [for] for, [against] against, [abstain] abstain

Result. Motion [passed / failed]

4. Approval of previous minutes

Document. Minutes of [date]

Motion. Approve those minutes [as presented / with these corrections: ...].

Moved by [name]. Seconded by [name].

Vote. [for] for, [against] against, [abstain] abstain

Result. Motion [passed / failed]

5. Reports

[Officer or committee]: [What they reported, in one or two sentences.]

A report is not a decision. If the board voted, record that motion in the next section.

6. Decisions

Copy this block once for each vote.

Item. [short title]

Motion. "[Exact words the board voted on.]"

Moved by [name]. Seconded by [name].

Vote. [for] for, [against] against, [abstain] abstain

Names, if the vote was not unanimous. [no votes and abstentions, or "unanimous"]

Result. Motion [passed / failed]

7. Action items

  • [Task]. Assigned to [name]. Due [date].

8. Owner comments

[One sentence per topic, if the board held an open forum. Leave this section out if there was no comment period.]

9. Next meeting

[Date, time, and location, or "Not set."]

10. Adjournment

Motion. Adjourn.

Moved by [name]. Seconded by [name].

Vote. [result]

The meeting adjourned at [time].

Approved by the board on [date].

Formatting aid for volunteer secretaries. Not legal advice. Follow your governing documents and state law, and ask your association's attorney if you are unsure what to record.
