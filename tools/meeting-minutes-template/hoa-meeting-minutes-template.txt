HOA MEETING MINUTES

Association: [Association name]
Meeting: [Board meeting / annual meeting / special meeting]
Date: [month day, year]
Location: [address, video link, or both]
Called to order: [time] by [name, role]
Adjourned: [time]
Minutes by: [name, role]

Draft for the board to review. These minutes become the record when the board approves them.


1. Call to order

[Name, role] called the meeting to order at [time].


2. Attendance and quorum

Directors present: [names]
Directors absent: [names]
Also present: [owners or guests, or "none"]
Quorum: A quorum [was / was not] present under the bylaws.


3. Approval of the agenda

Motion: Approve the agenda [as presented / with these changes: ...].
Moved by: [name]
Seconded by: [name]
Vote: [for] for, [against] against, [abstain] abstain
Result: Motion [passed / failed]


4. Approval of previous minutes

Document: Minutes of [date]
Motion: Approve those minutes [as presented / with these corrections: ...].
Moved by: [name]
Seconded by: [name]
Vote: [for] for, [against] against, [abstain] abstain
Result: Motion [passed / failed]


5. Reports

[Officer or committee]: [What they reported, in one or two sentences.]

A report is not a decision. If the board voted, record that motion in the next section.


6. Decisions

Copy this block once for each vote.

Item: [short title]
Motion: "[Exact words the board voted on.]"
Moved by: [name]
Seconded by: [name]
Vote: [for] for, [against] against, [abstain] abstain
Names, if the vote was not unanimous: [no votes and abstentions, or "unanimous"]
Result: Motion [passed / failed]


7. Action items

- [Task]. Assigned to [name]. Due [date].


8. Owner comments

[One sentence per topic, if the board held an open forum. Leave this section out if there was no comment period.]


9. Next meeting

[Date, time, and location, or "Not set."]


10. Adjournment

Motion: Adjourn.
Moved by: [name]
Seconded by: [name]
Vote: [result]
The meeting adjourned at [time].


Approved by the board on [date].

---
Formatting aid for volunteer secretaries. Not legal advice. Follow your governing documents and state law, and ask your association's attorney if you are unsure what to record.
