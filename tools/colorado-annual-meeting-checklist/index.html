Colorado HOA annual meeting checklist

A before, during, and after list for the secretary of a small self-managed association. It is a way to stay organized. Checking a box does not mean the meeting met your documents or Colorado law.

This is general organizational guidance for a volunteer secretary. It is not legal advice and it is not a compliance checklist. Quorum, notice, elections, proxies, and records depend on your governing documents and on state law, including the Colorado Common Interest Ownership Act (CCIOA). Confirm those rules with your association's attorney.
