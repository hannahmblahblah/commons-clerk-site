Free tools for volunteer HOA boards

Printable help for the secretary who got the minutes: a meeting minutes template, a meeting notice, and a Colorado annual meeting checklist. The notice form runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent to us. These pages are organizational aids, not legal advice.

HOA meeting minutes template

A blank you can print or copy, with room for the call to order, quorum, each motion with the mover, the second, and the vote, action items, and adjournment.

HOA meeting notice template

Enter the association, meeting type, date, time, place or video link, and agenda. Copy or print a notice for a board, annual, or special meeting.
